# TOKENS

## Principle
Defines the rules and semantic reasoning for this aspect of visual intelligence.

## Why it exists
To prevent arbitrary aesthetic decisions and root them in semantic meaning.

## When to use
Use when instructed by the Visual Intent model.

## When NOT to use
Do not apply arbitrarily to "make things pop".

## Tokens and Components
Relies exclusively on Phase 2 design tokens and Phase 3/4 components.

## Common AI Mistakes
- Adding effects instead of relying on typography and spacing.
- Creating variants instead of composing patterns.

## AI Decision Making
1. Consult the page objective.
2. Follow the Art Direction rules for the chosen visual mode.
3. Apply existing tokens.
