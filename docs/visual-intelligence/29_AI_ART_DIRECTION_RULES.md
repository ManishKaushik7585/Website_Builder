# AI ART DIRECTION RULES

## The Decision Framework
1. Understand page objective
2. Identify audience
3. Identify brand personality
4. Identify emotional / functional intent
5. Select visual mode
6. Establish primary focal point
7. Establish secondary focal points
8. Determine content density
9. Determine typography hierarchy
10. Determine color hierarchy
11. Determine media direction
12. Determine section rhythm
13. Determine composition
14. Determine interaction intensity
15. Determine motion intensity
16. Determine responsive composition
17. Validate accessibility
18. Remove unnecessary decoration
19. Generate PageConfig
20. Perform final visual QA
